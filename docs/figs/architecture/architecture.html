Example Insurance Claims Dialogue

Pending documents

Claim CL1001   pending_docs
Product: Hospitalization Medical A
Uploaded: ID card
Missing: discharge summary, invoice
SLA: 3–5 days after docs complete
Q: What exactly is missing? How long until I get paid?
A: ID is in. Please send the discharge summary and original invoice. Review then takes 3–5 days — I cannot promise an exact payout date.

Rejection dispute

Claim CL2002   rejected
Reason: waiting-period / term mismatch
Safe: explain terms, file reconsideration
Forbidden: invent coverage, promise pay
Q: Why was this denied? I want it paid today.
A: I can open a reconsideration ticket and cite the clause. I cannot override the rejection or guarantee a payout.
